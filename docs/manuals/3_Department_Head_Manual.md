# User Manual: Department Head

> **Role Key:** `department_head`  
> **Access Scope:** Departmental governance, receiving and responding to targeted circulars, managing departmental equipment repairs, and reviewing department calendar schedules.

---

## 1. Introduction

As a **Department Head** (e.g., Head of Mathematics, Science, or English), you serve as the official operational link between school executive leadership and your faculty team.

You are responsible for:

- Acknowledging and responding to administrative circulars on behalf of your department.
- Disseminating official school directives to your teachers.
- Reporting facility or technical issues in departmental laboratories, classrooms, and offices.
- Coordinating departmental meetings and academic exam milestones.

---

## 2. Phase 1: First-Time Login & Profile Setup

### Step 1: Accessing Your Account

1. You will receive your initial login credentials directly from the School Administrator.
2. Navigate to `/login`. Enter your email and temporary password.
3. Click **Sign In**.

### Step 2: Verifying Departmental Affiliation

1. Upon logging in, examine the top header bar next to your name.
2. You should see your official **Department Badge** (e.g., `[قسم الرياضيات]` or `[Science Department]`).
3. If your department affiliation is missing or incorrect, contact the School Administrator to update your record in `/settings/departments`.

### Step 3: Updating Your Password

1. If you ever forget your password, click the **Forgot password?** (`نسيت كلمة المرور؟`) link on the login page.
2. Enter your registered email address to receive an instant, secure reset link.
3. Choose a strong new password and confirm it.

## 3. Phase 2: Daily Operations & Core Workflows

### 1. Your Personalized Operational Dashboard (`/dashboard`)

Your homepage at `/dashboard` is custom-tailored to your department:

- **Active Circulars Requiring Action:** Circulars addressed specifically to your department or school-wide that are pending your response.
- **Department Maintenance Tickets:** Outstanding work orders submitted for your department's rooms or equipment.
- **Quick Actions:** Instant buttons to respond to circulars, submit maintenance requests, or open the calendar.

---

### 2. Handling Administrative Circulars (`/circulars`)

The portal delivers all official administrative directives with legally verifiable tracking.

#### Receiving a New Circular:

1. When the School Administration issues a circular targeting your department, you receive an instant **Real-Time Push Notification** (a Sonner toast appears in the corner with a direct link).
2. The **Notification Bell** icon in the header displays an updated unread counter badge.

#### Reading & Reviewing Official Attachments:

1. Click the notification or navigate to **Administrative Circulars** (`/circulars`).
2. Open the circular.
3. Review the instructions, reference number, priority, and submission deadline.
4. If official files are attached (PDF schedules, Ministry guidelines, exam templates):
   - Click **View Document** for an instant in-browser preview.
   - Click **Download** to save the document to your device.
   - Click **Print Document** to print a clean copy directly.

#### Submitting Your Official Department Response:

Depending on what the School Administrator required:

- **Acknowledgement Only:** Click **Confirm Acknowledgment** (`تأكيد الاطلاع والاستلام`) to log your official receipt.
- **Written Response / Plan Submission:**
  1. In the response text box, enter your department's feedback, assigned faculty leads, or implementation notes.
  2. Click **Submit Response** (`إرسال الرد`).

3. Your submission is recorded with an immutable timestamp and immediately marked as completed on the Principal's compliance monitor.

---

### 3. Reporting Equipment & Facility Issues (`/maintenance/new`)

When computers in a lab, air conditioners in departmental classrooms, or office furniture need repair:

1. In the sidebar, navigate to **Maintenance > New Request** (`/maintenance/new`).
2. Fill out the request:
   - **Title:** Clear summary (e.g., `عطل في جهاز العرض الضوئي بمختبر الفيزياء`).
   - **Category:** Electrical, HVAC, Carpentry, Plumbing, or IT Equipment.
   - **Location:** Select your departmental space from the dropdown (e.g., `مختبر الفيزياء`).
   - **Priority:** Mark `Urgent` if teaching is actively disrupted.
   - **Description:** Specific symptoms of the problem.
   - **Attach Photo:** Take a picture with your phone or upload a snapshot of the issue (JPG, PNG up to 10MB).
3. Click **Submit Request**.
4. You can track the technician's progress directly on the ticket page as it transitions from `assigned` to `in_progress` to `completed`.

---

### 4. Viewing the Academic & Exam Calendar (`/calendar`)

1. Click **School Calendar** in the navigation bar.
2. Toggle between **Month View** and **List View**.
3. Filter by categories such as **Exam**, **Academic Schedule**, or **Administrative Meeting**.
4. Click on any event to view the full details, venue, start/end times, and organizer notes.
